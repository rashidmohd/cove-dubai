About Cove

48 rooms.
One address.

In the quieter part of a city that rarely slows down.

Forty-eight rooms. Three dining spaces. A spa, a salon, a fitness floor — and a kitchen that earns its place at the centre of it all. Cove is not a large hotel made to feel intimate. It was always meant to be this size.

Dubai is outside. Inside, time moves differently.
